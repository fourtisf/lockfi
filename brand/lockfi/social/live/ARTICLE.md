Title: LockFi Is Live: Liquidity That Stays, Numbers That Don't Lie

LockFi is live on Robinhood Chain. Here is what it does, what it will never do, and why we built it.
## The problem we kept seeing
Every new chain goes through the same cycle. Tokens launch fast, liquidity arrives fast, and it leaves faster. Dashboards show yields in the thousands of percent, computed from one good day. Platforms hold user funds in contracts nobody can read. Rewards are paid in a token printed for the purpose, and when the printing slows, so does everything else.
The people who pay for that cycle are liquidity providers. They are the ones who make a market tradable, and they are usually the last to find out what the numbers really meant.
## Our mission
LockFi exists to make providing liquidity on Robinhood Chain honest, simple and safe enough to do without trusting us.
Three things follow from that, and they are not negotiable:
**You keep custody.** Every position is minted through Uniswap's own audited contracts, straight to your wallet, as an NFT only you control. LockFi holds nothing. You can withdraw whenever you want, on LockFi or on Uniswap itself.
**Rewards come from real trading fees only.** No emissions, no printed rewards, no subsidised figures. If trading slows, what you earn slows with it, and the site says so.
**Every number tells you where it came from.** A figure on LockFi says its source and its age. A pool younger than a week is marked as an estimate. Under a day of data, we show a dash instead of a number. Out of range means earning nothing, and the page says it in red.
## What is live today
**Pools.** Every market on Robinhood Chain that clears our listing bar, ranked and updated live: market cap, liquidity, volume, buys and sells.
**Positions.** Build a concentrated liquidity position in one screen. Pick a shape (spot, curve or bid-ask), a range and a deposit, and see where your liquidity sits against the current price before you sign.
**One token in.** You do not need to hold both sides of a pair. LockFi swaps the right share in the same Uniswap pool, then mints. Every transaction is simulated before your wallet asks you to sign.
**Portfolio.** Every position your wallet holds, read from the chain: in range or out of range, fees earned, and price impact against simply holding. Collect fees or withdraw in one click.
**Ask LockFi AI.** Ask about any pool or position and get a plain answer based on the figures on the page. It explains; it never predicts prices and never tells you what to do.
## What is coming next
**LockFi Router.** A tool for token teams to turn creator fees into permanent liquidity in their own pool, on a schedule, with price guards on every route. We are finishing its security review now, and it launches only when that review is done.
## What LockFi will never do
Take custody of your funds.
Pay you in a token we printed.
Show you a yield annualised from a single day and call it a promise.
Hide the risks.
## The risks, plainly
Concentrated liquidity can lose value against simply holding when the price moves. That is price impact, and your portfolio shows it next to your fees.
A position out of range earns nothing until the price returns or you rebalance.
Fees depend on trading. Past fees are not a promise of future ones.
Start small, read what the page tells you, and never put in more than you can afford to lose.
